⚖ XP SCORING GUIDE

Every quest's XP comes from three dials. The evaluator multiplies them so big, hard, goal-critical tasks pay far more than busywork.

EFFORT — how taxing is it?
Trivial ×1 · Light ×2 · Moderate ×3 · Hard ×5 · Brutal ×8

TIME — how long?
<15m ×1 · 15–45m ×2 · 1–2h ×3 · Half-day ×5 · Multi-day ×8

IMPACT — does it move a real goal (5K, SAT, Tokyo)?
Low ×1 · Medium ×1.5 · High ×2.2 · Pivotal ×3

XP = (EFFORT + TIME) × IMPACT × 5

Quick rule of thumb if you'd rather not fiddle:
• tiny habit ≈ 10 · solid task ≈ 30–50 · hard half-day ≈ 80–120 · multi-day grind ≈ 150–250

A full day of dailies is ~200 XP, so price NPC bounties against that — a 100-XP bounty should feel like half a day well spent.
